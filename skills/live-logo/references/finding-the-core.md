# Finding the core and the origin

The **core** is the part of the logo that carries the brand on its own. The **origin** is the point or edge the motion grows from. Get these two right and almost any style will feel like it belongs to the logo.

## Finding the core

Work through these questions with the logo in front of you. The first clear answer wins.

1. **Is there one distinctive feature?** A cut, an arrow hidden in negative space, a dot over a letter, a swoosh, a bite, a slanted stroke, a spark. That is the core.
2. **Is there a symbol next to a name?** The symbol is the core; the name follows it.
3. **Is it initials or one letter?** The letter's most characteristic stroke (the diagonal of a Z, the bowl of a P, the crossbar of an A) is the core.
4. **Is it a wordmark only?** Look for the special letter: a custom ligature, a different colour, a letter that breaks the pattern. If none, the first letter is the core, and the motion follows reading order.
5. **Is it an emblem or badge?** The outer shape is the frame and the central picture or initials are the core. The frame usually arrives first and the core lands inside it.
6. **Is it a mascot?** The face, and especially the eyes, are the core. A blink or a look is often all the motion it needs.

Check with the analyser: the biggest part and the part nearest the centre are often the core, but not always. A thin accent stroke in the brand colour can matter more than a large block.

## Finding the origin

| What you see | Origin | Motion that follows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Looks the same turned upside down (S, Z, N, pinwheels) | Centre point | Halves split or unfold from the centre; spin into place |
| Mirror image left to right (A, M, V, W, shields, many symbols) | Vertical centre line | Centre wipe; both halves slide in from the sides or open from the middle |
| Mirror image top to bottom (E-like bars, stacked lines) | Horizontal centre line | Lines open up and down from the middle |
| Radial (stars, sparks, suns, flowers, asterisks) | Centre point | Rays grow outward, rotate, pulse; one ray flickers |
| Circular (rings, wheels, globes, O-shaped marks) | Centre, around the rim | Rotate in, the ring draws around, a dot orbits and lands |
| Grows from a base (buildings, trees, mountains, bars, flames) | Bottom edge | Rise and grow upward, staggered from left to right |
| Hangs or is heavy at the top (drops, pins, stamps, umbrellas) | Top edge | Drops in with a small settle; stamp down |
| A name in a left-to-right script | Left edge | Letters arrive in reading order; mask wipe left to right; typing |
| A name in a right-to-left script | Right edge | The same, mirrored |
| One continuous line (signatures, line art, monoline marks) | The line's start point | Draw on along the path, then fill |
| Separate pieces (dots, tiles, blocks, puzzle parts) | Outside, gathering inward | Pieces fly, fall or orbit into position |
| One dominant shape with small details | The dominant shape | It appears first, details pop on after |
| Overlapping shapes (interlocking rings, layered leaves) | The shape at the back | Layers stack from back to front |
| A shape with a clear direction (arrows, chevrons, wings, speed lines) | Behind the arrow | Moves in the direction it points |

## Reading personality

| Personality | Signs | Motion character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calm, premium | Lots of space, thin type, muted colours | Slow ease-out, small distances, fades and blurs, no overshoot |
| Precise, technical | Geometric, monospace, grids, hard edges | Clean draw-ons, wipes, stepped or snapping moves, exact timing |
| Playful | Rounded shapes, bright colours, mascots | Springs, squash and stretch, bounces, wiggles |
| Bold, loud | Heavy type, high contrast, big colour blocks | Fast, large scale changes, hard cuts, kinetic type |
| Organic, human | Hand-drawn, curves, natural colours | Growing, morphing, handwriting, soft ease-in-out |
| Trustworthy, steady (finance, health, law, government) | Conservative type, blues and greens, seals | One quiet gesture, fades, no bounce, nothing that feels unstable |
